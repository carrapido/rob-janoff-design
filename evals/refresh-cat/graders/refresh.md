The final response refreshes the cat logo without losing what people recognize. All of these hold:
1. It starts from a short critique and states what must survive (the cat's silhouette: head, ears, fringe).
2. It offers three options of growing change: a rebuild of the same shape on the golden series, a refinement that also fixes the critique's faults (for example widening the gaps that vanish at 48 px), and a rethink of one element while the silhouette stays.
3. Each option is shown or described beside the original, so the change is visible.
4. It recommends one option and ends by asking the client to pick before building a full preview.
