The final response shows three concepts for Tierra Alta and stops at a checkpoint. All of these hold:
1. Exactly three concepts are presented, and they are genuinely different (for example an essential silhouette with a cut, a negative-space wink, and a continuous line or monogram), not three variations of one idea. A short "Also considered" line naming runners-up does not count as a concept.
2. Each concept comes with a one-sentence idea, its wink (the detail that makes the viewer smile) and one honest risk.
3. The concepts are drawn in one ink (a solid dark silhouette), not in full color.
4. The response recommends one concept and says why.
5. The response ends by asking the client to choose (or say what they like in each) before anything else is built; it does not deliver a full color preview, a scale test page or a context test yet.
