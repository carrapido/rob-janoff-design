The final response is a critique of the cat logo, not a redesign. All of these hold:
1. It opens with a one-sentence verdict.
2. It says what people recognize and must survive (for example the cat's head and ears silhouette, the fringe, the hood).
3. It lists what fails, most serious first, each with evidence. It must include that the thin gaps around the hair (about 10 units in a 700-unit frame) drop under 1 px at 48 px and vanish.
4. It judges the logo against Janoff's golden rules (simplicity, homework, passing trends, balance in its environment, a wink and a smile), one line each.
5. It ends by offering a refresh instead of delivering one.
